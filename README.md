🤰 NEXMUM — GrabDoctor Maternal Health Companion
Your all-in-one pregnancy companion — from home visits to nutrition, every step of your journey covered.

🎯 Problem Statement
Pregnant women in Malaysia face a fragmented healthcare experience. 
There is currently no single platform that supports the full pregnancy journey from accessing at-home 
medical care and tracking vitals, to understanding nutrition needs and following trimester-
specific health guidance. This gap leaves mothers especially first-timers that navigating 
multiple disconnected apps, clinics, and Google searches, often without culturally relevant support.

This challenge directly impacts outcomes tied to UN Sustainable Development Goal 3 
(Good Health & Well-Being) and WHO maternal health priorities.

💡 Solution
NEXMUM is a maternal health super-app built for Malaysian mothers. It brings together 
everything a pregnant woman needs into one beautifully designed, bilingual (English & 
Bahasa Malaysia) mobile platform:
1. 🏠 GrabDoc
   Book home-visit doctors, OBGYNs, and certified midwives. Track
   appointments end-to-end and receive e-prescriptions with direct medication delivery.
2. 🤖 AI Report Reader
   Upload blood test PDFs and receive instant AI-powered analysis in plain language.
3. 💬 AI Maternity Chatbot
   Ask anything pregnancy-related and get intelligent, context-aware answers.
4. 🥗 Smart Meal Planner
   Log meals, track calories, macros, iron, calcium, and folic acid.
   Get AI-generated weekly meal plans tailored to your trimester, with a Modern
   Confinement (Pantang) guide.
5. 🤰 Ibu Sihat Hub
   Week-by-week baby development updates, trimester checklists,
   a baby kick counter, safe exercise guides, and symptom management.
6. 📊 Vitals Dashboard
   Log blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, and SpO₂.
   Sync with Apple Watch, Fitbit, Samsung Health, and Omron BP monitors.
7. 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Sync
   Share pregnancy updates and health data with your partner and family.
