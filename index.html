Common Symptoms
What to expect at Week 24
😌 Back Pain
Common as your centre of gravity shifts. Use a supportive pillow and do gentle stretches.
Normal
⚠️ Round Ligament Pain
Sharp pain in lower abdomen or flank when changing positions. This is normal — the ligaments supporting your uterus are stretching.
Normal
😌 Braxton Hicks
Mild, irregular tightenings — your body's practice contractions. They should be painless and irregular. Rest and stay hydrated.
Normal
🚨 Sudden Swelling / Severe Headache
May signal preeclampsia. Contact Dr. Aisha immediately if you experience sudden swelling, blurred vision, or severe headache.

Baby Tips
Week 24 development insights
🧠 Brain Development
Your baby's brain is growing rapidly this week. The taste buds are now active — your baby can taste what you eat through the amniotic fluid! Try eating a varied, nutritious diet.
👂 Hearing & Senses
Your baby can now hear sounds from outside the womb. Talk, sing, or play soft music — they will recognise your voice after birth!
🎵 Tip: Classical music or lullabies played gently can be soothing for your baby now.
😊 Face & Features
All facial features are fully formed — eyelids, eyebrows, and tiny lips are visible. Your baby's eyes can now open and close.
